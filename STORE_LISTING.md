# FocusLock — Chrome Web Store listing (draft)

## Short description (132 chars max)
Timed focus sessions. Your study tabs stay open; Shorts, feeds and doomscroll sites wait until you're done.

## Full description
FocusLock is a study guard for students and knowledge workers who keep
drifting from a lecture into Shorts, Instagram, Reddit, or "one quick check".

HOW IT WORKS
1. Open your lecture or work tab and pin it as a study tab.
2. Start a session (25, 50, 90 or 120 minutes, or your own length).
3. Study. Switching between pinned study tabs is always free. Anything
   else is paused with a calm screen and a way back.

YOUTUBE STUDY GUARD — NOT A YOUTUBE BLOCKER
Your lectures stay open: watch pages, playlists, search results, live
streams, embeds and channels. Only Shorts, the home feed, trending,
explore, gaming and podcasts pause during a session.

PARK URGES, DON'T FIGHT THEM
Type "check Instagram" into the popup instead of opening it. It's saved
for after the session — the impulse passes, the thought doesn't get lost.

BREAKS THAT ACTUALLY REST YOU
Long sessions include a real break: step away from the screen. Skipping
is allowed and noted honestly in your summary.

PRIVACY
100% local. No account, no analytics, no tracking, no servers. Everything
(study tabs, urges, session history, settings) lives in chrome.storage.local
on your device. Uninstalling deletes it all.

PERMISSIONS (and why)
- tabs: pin study tabs, return you to them from pause screens
- storage: keep session/settings/history locally
- alarms: end phases on time with the popup closed
- notifications: break + completion reminders only
- webNavigation + all-URLs access: detect navigations (including
  single-page-app moves on YouTube/Instagram/X) so pauses apply reliably.
  Page content is never collected.

FocusLock creates friction, not guilt. It won't trap you: every pause
screen has a way back to study, a way to park the urge, and — if you
enabled it — a deliberate 2-minute emergency pass with a logged reason.
